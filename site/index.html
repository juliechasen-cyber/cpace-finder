      How this was built

      Twelve states have had their rules read out of program guidelines, statutes, or administrator FAQs, and each lists the people who actually run the program. Every other state carries volume data only, and says so rather than showing a plausible guess.

      Volume figures come from the C-PACE Alliance national program volume table, which still reports 2024 as its most recent year. Morningstar DBRS has since put 2025 PACE origination near $4.5 billion, over 96% of it C-PACE, against $2.42 billion in 2024. Treat the totals here as a floor.

      Where two official sources contradict each other, both are shown with a note on which one governs. That has already come up twice, in Pennsylvania and Minnesota, and in both cases the stale source is a government agency.

      Not attempted yet: county-level coverage. That is the most useful layer and the most expensive, and it needs participating-jurisdiction lists from each administrator rather than a state-level field.
